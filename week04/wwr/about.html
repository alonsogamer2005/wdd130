Perú Rivers! Make Memories!

Our goal is to provide you with the best rafting experience possible in Peru. We have been providing unforgettable adventures across Andean rivers for over 30 years. We are a family-owned and operated business dedicated to safety, excitement, and showcasing Peru's breathtaking natural landscapes.

History

Perú Expeditions started navigating the rushing waters of the Lunahuaná and Urubamba rivers in 1995. What began as a small local passion has grown into Peru's premier whitewater adventure company.

We take pride in employing experienced local guides who know every current and rapid inside out, ensuring every traveler enjoys a thrilling, safe, and culturally rich journey through Peru's canyons.
